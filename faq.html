Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the Bloomfield Renaissance Faire?

A: See the "About" section on our Home page.

Q: When is the Renaissance Faire?

A: See times for The Big Day (our main event) and events happening at the library on our Events page.

Q: Where is the Renaissance Faire located?

A: See the google map for the Big Day on the Events page.

Q: What is the price of admission and parking?

A: Free.

Q: Do events cost?

A: Any event with a craft material associated with it does cost. Prices should be clearly posted.

Q: What payment types do you accept?

A: Cash only.

Q: Are there restrooms?

A: The courthouse will have restrooms open during the event.

Q: What's the difference between Big day events and Library events?

A: Library events are held on the weekdays and are aimed towards children. These are free. Big day events are geared towards a broader audience and are often paid events.

Q: What happens in the event of a bad weather, a delay or a cancellation?

A: The best source of information is Facebook. In case of bad weather, we have a rain date scheduled for Saturday July 31, 2027.

Q: I want to volunteer, who do I contact?

A: The best method is bloomfieldrenfaire@gmail.com
